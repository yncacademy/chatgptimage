    Pro tips

    Twenty habits that make prompts work harder

    The small choices — purpose first, subject before style, one change at a time — are what separate a usable image from a lucky one.

    • 1. Start with the purpose of the image. Say whether it is for a profile photo, product page, blog post, poster, presentation, social post, or another use.

    • 2. Describe the main subject before adding style words. ChatGPT needs to know what must be visible.

    • 3. Explain the action. Say what the person, animal, product, or object is doing.

    • 4. Add the setting. A kitchen, studio, street, classroom, workshop, beach, or office creates a different visual result.

    • 5. Use composition words such as close-up, full body, overhead view, wide scene, three-quarter view, centered subject, or empty space on the left.

    • 6. Describe light in a practical way. Try soft window light, overcast daylight, warm sunset light, direct side light, or controlled studio light.

    • 7. Mention textures when they matter. Include fabric, wood, glass, paper, stone, metal, skin, food, foliage, or water.

    • 8. Tell ChatGPT what to preserve. For a person, mention identity, facial features, skin texture, hairstyle, and natural proportions.

    • 9. For a product, mention shape, color, packaging, labels, proportions, and material. Ask ChatGPT not to invent a logo or change the product design.

    • 10. Keep text inside an image short. Put the exact wording in quotation marks and say where it should appear.

    • 11. Ask for no extra text when you need a clean design. This helps reduce unwanted labels and random writing.

    • 12. For diagrams, name every label and explain the correct order or relationship between parts.

    • 13. For historical scenes, give the place and time period. Ask for appropriate clothing, tools, architecture, transport, and materials.

    • 14. Use an uploaded reference photo when likeness matters. Make sure you have permission to use the person's image.

    • 15. Do not change five things at once during an edit. Fix the biggest problem first, then review the result.

    • 16. Request the right layout for the platform. A portrait image works well for many mobile posts, while a wide image may suit a website banner.

    • 17. Leave space for text when the image will include a headline. Say which side should remain simple and uncluttered.

    • 18. Use specific details instead of a long list of vague adjectives. Clear instructions are easier to follow than words such as amazing, beautiful, or perfect.

    • 19. Check hands, faces, labels, signs, diagrams, and small objects before publishing. A good overall image can still have one visible mistake.

    • 20. Save your strongest prompts in a personal library. Change the subject, setting, colors, and purpose to create new versions.

    The truth about long prompts

    A longer prompt is not automatically a better prompt.

    The useful part of a prompt is not the number of fancy words. It is the clear direction. ChatGPT needs to understand the image's job, the main subject, the visible details, the composition, and the limits.

    The first result may not be the final result. Image creation works best as a short conversation. Start with the main idea, look at the result, then ask for one focused change.

    Reference images can help with identity, clothing, products, and layout, but they do not remove the need to describe what should change and what should stay the same.

    Text in images should be checked carefully. Keep the wording short, use exact quotation marks, and review every letter before using the image in public.

    The strongest prompt is the one that helps you get the right image for a real purpose.

    Good image prompts are clear, specific, and connected to a real use case.

    Tell ChatGPT what to create, what the viewer should notice, how the scene should be arranged, and what must remain accurate. Then improve the result one step at a time.
